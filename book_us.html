Book Us!

To inquire about our shows and services, call us at 708-704-9911. We can attend a wide variety of venues, such as: youth centers, schools, park districts, and more! We teach about exotics' ideal environments, care requirements, common medical issues, the environment, and much more. The shows and booths are tailored to the learning level of the guests attending the event.
